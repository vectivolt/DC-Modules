## 14. Simulation-fidelity decisions

| # | Decision | Value | Why | Status | Held by · invalidator |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 14.1 | LLC decks | power-solved per-SKU ngspice decks with body diodes, the star held at mid-bus, a physicality guard and a tank fingerprint; acceptance is mode correctness, ZVS, stress and capability margin — **not** point power near resonance, which is FHA-limited | a deck without body diodes put the legs at ±6 kV on a 650 V bus, and every number it produced was non-physical | FROZEN | `spice/llc/llc-run.mjs` · `current-coordination` fails on a stale or unphysical deck · a tank change |
| 14.2 | PFC models | cycle-by-cycle Vienna in JavaScript with catalogue L(i), a floating neutral and Σi = 0 imposed under the DCM clamp, cross-checked against the Friedli–Kolar closed forms within 0.7 %; an averaged-switch model for line-cycle and THD runs with the switching ripple handled analytically | a switched model that lets the three phase currents stop summing to zero runs away at no load, and cycle-by-cycle three-phase PWM over a line cycle is numerically infeasible | FROZEN | `pfc/vienna-switched.mjs` · `spice/pfc/pfc-phase-run.mjs` · `verify-independent` research anchors |
| 14.3 | Double pulse | per-SKU decks at the **real** turn-off currents on the **drawn** networks, with the shipped gate bias and a corrected output-charge curve; every CSV fingerprinted and read by a gate | evidence run at 78 A and 25 A against real 95–165 A is not evidence, and nothing was reading it | FROZEN | `spice/double-pulse/dpt-run.mjs` · `stress-audit` `[DPT]` · a loop, snubber or gate-resistor change |
| 14.4 | DC link | its own deck with the entry film, the stud loop and the damper, swept over film count and stud inductance | the link was never in a netlist, and every can was carrying 5.8–12 A rms against a ≈ 3 A class | FROZEN | `spice/dclink/dclink-ripple.mjs` · `current-coordination` `[DCLINK]`/`[DCLINK-VIENNA]` · T-57 |
| 14.5 | EMI | the LISN solve is pre-compliance **tendency** only, never claimed as compliance | a SPICE LISN cannot substitute for a chamber | FROZEN | `emi/lisn-precompliance.mjs` · a lab scan |
| 14.6 | Magnetics | flux, core and copper loss and a two-node thermal network integrated from the real waveforms at every corner; PyOpenMagnetics is a **second opinion**, not evidence; 2-D field solvers are for first-article fringing and leakage | one model's output is not proof — the cross-check exists to disagree, and where it does (D3 copper ×1.29–1.32) the class lines are re-checked at its number | FROZEN | `magnetics-envelope` · `magnetics/mkf-crosscheck.py` · a construction change without re-running the cross-check |
| 14.7 | Host plants | a plant kinder than the hardware hides defects, so the host models row-granular program-once flash, a bounded watchdog, real comparator polarity and references, flat-topped mains and AC-coupled sensors | the plants are part of the design, not scaffolding around it | FROZEN | `firmware/test/*` · `firmware/run_tests.sh` under ASan/UBSan |
| 14.8 | Gates read results | every gate reads the result file its engine wrote; a hand-copied constant, or a check that cannot fail, is a defect | a gate that asserts a literal proves only that somebody typed it | FROZEN | `evidence.mjs` · `review-checks` · `fw-constants-sync` |
