> **Historical record — the answer to external review R3 (2026-09-06), kept verbatim.** The review covered the
> exported 30 / 60 / 120 kW PDFs; its pin-numbering findings were answered claim by claim.
> **Where it lives now:** the generated [symbol → package pin map](../symbol-pin-map.md) · register A6 rev R3 · the
> later R4–R8 rounds in register rows E45–E49.

An external reviewer audited the exported 30/60/120 kW schematic PDFs and returned a
"do not manufacture" verdict, principally on **component pin numbering**. Every finding was
checked against the built netlist and, where it turned on a part, against the manufacturer
datasheet (24 verification agents, adversarial second pass on each claimed mismatch).

**Summary: the reviewer was right about the drawings and wrong about the netlist — but the
audit also surfaced six real defects we did not know about, one of them critical.**

---

## 1. The central finding: logical vs physical pins

The reviewer's headline claim is that the MOSFETs, drivers, controllers and regulators carry
pin numbers that do not match the physical devices — e.g. `B3M010C075Z` drawn as
`1=G, 2=D, 3=S, 4=KS`.

**That is exactly what the PDFs show, and it is not what the netlist contains.**

The design is authored with *logical* pin identifiers because the cells are SKU-generic. Every
netlist that leaves the repo is emitted through a remap
(`calculations/easyeda-apply-gen.mjs:54`) that translates them to physical package pins:

| Part | Drawn (logical) | Built (physical) | Datasheet | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B3M010C075Z | 1=G 2=D 3=S | **4=G 1=D 2=S 3=Kelvin** | 1=D(tab) 2=Power S 3=Kelvin S 4=G | **MATCHES** |
| SG2M023120LJ | 1=G 2=D 3=S | **4=G 1=D 2=S 3=Kelvin** | identical | **MATCHES** |
| TPS54202 | 1=VIN 2=GND 3=SW | **3=VIN 1=GND 2=SW** | 1=GND 2=SW 3=VIN | **MATCHES** |
| NCP1252A | 3=FB 2=GND | **1=FB 5=GND 6=DRV** | 1=FB 2=BO 3=CS 5=GND | **MATCHES** |
| TLV9061 | — | **1=OUT 2=V- 3=IN+ 4=IN- 5=V+** | identical | **MATCHES** |
| VOM1271T | — | **1=A1 2=C1 3=C2 4=A2** | identical | **MATCHES** |
| TLP152 | 8=VCC | **1=A 3=C 4=GND 5=VO 6=VCC** | identical | **MATCHES** |
| NSI6611 | 1=VIA 14=KSRC | **1=ASC 2=DESAT … 15=VCC1 16=TEST** | identical (15/16 pins) | 1 defect, §3 |

The reviewer's own qualification was correct: *"a generator can use logical pin identifiers
internally, provided a verified mapping translates them to the correct physical pads. That
mapping is not supplied here."* It existed but had never been published. It now is:
**[`docs/symbol-pin-map.md`](../symbol-pin-map.md)** — 107 part families, 27 carrying a remap,
407 pins, generated by `calculations/pin-map-export.mjs`.

A genuine hazard the audit did surface: **BASiC's TO-247-4 pinout is not the Western one.**
Wolfspeed / onsemi / Microchip TO-247-4 SiC parts use `1=G 2=D 3=S 4=Kelvin`. Any symbol reused
from those vendors would be wrong here. Ours is correct for BASiC; the footprint must not be
inherited from a Wolfspeed library part.

## 2. Claims that are false — PDF reading artifacts

Checked directly against the built netlist:

| Claim | Reality |
|---|---|
| "VAC1 and VAC2 both measure AC2" | **False.** `RV1D0→AC1`, `RV2D0→AC2`, `RV3D0→AC3`. |
| "`PWM_A0/B0/C0` are detached stubs" | **False.** All three are on MCU pins. |
| "`PWM_A1`, `PWM_B1` unconnected" | **False.** Absent at 30 kW (one Vienna cell); present and driven at 60/120 kW. |
| "`SNS_VMID` has no MCU connection" | **False.** On an MCU pin. |
| "Both shunt Kelvin inputs share `USHO_VINN`" | **False.** `USHO.VINP↔RSHO.KA` and `USHO.VINN↔RSHO.KB` are separate traces; the third tie is the isolated-common reference, which is intended. |
| "Resonance is 185.5 kHz from 4×46 nF + 4 µH" | **False.** 4.0 µH is the *trim* inductor. E7 rev D2 freezes **Lr(total) = 7.0 µH = 3 µH transformer leakage + 4.0 µH trim** (bin-selected against measured leakage), Cr 185 nF → **fr = 140 kHz, Zr = 6.15 Ω**. The reviewer flagged this possibility themselves. |
| "Precharge resistors have no value" | **Value exists** (33 Ω, `architecture.md`/`interconnect.md`); the drawing does not print it. |
| "Relay/fuse/pulse resistors undersized" | **Sized per SKU** in `skuOverrides` — the drawing printed the 30 kW label on all three SKUs. Fixed, §4. |
| "750 V device on an 800 V bus" | Not a blocker, as the reviewer also concluded — Vienna midpoint switch blocks ≈ half-bus. |

## 3. Real defects the audit found — CONFIRMED, adversarially re-checked

### 3.1 CRITICAL — GD32G553VET6 pin map is STM32G474-derived and wrong

`calculations/out/mcu-pinmap.csv` is headed `pin(symbolic)`; gate item **A6** records the map as
"on G474 conventions, GD32G553 datasheet check" — that check was never closed. Against
GD32G553xx Datasheet Rev 2.0 (Table 2-4, LQFP100):

| Signal | Ours | Actual pin 100 function | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `FLT_PFC` / `FLT_LLC` | 74 | **VSS** | **drives a fault output into ground** |
| `BOOT0` | 100 | **VDD** | **strap resistor shorts VDD to GND** |
| SWDIO | 28 | PA6 (ADC1_IN2) | part unprogrammable via SWD |
| SWCLK | 29 | PA7 | as above |
| VDDA/VREFP | ~19/20 | **VDDA=37, VREFP=36, VSSA=35** | analog supply unconnected |

Correct: VDD 24/49/64/75/100 · VSS 23/48/63/74/99 · VDDA 37 · VSSA 35 · VREFP 36 · VBAT 6 ·
NRST 14 · **BOOT0 95 (PB8, shared)** · **SWDIO 76 (PA13)** · **SWCLK 77 (PA14)**. There is no
VREFN pin (internally tied to VSSA). The part does have an HRTIMER and three advanced timers
(TIMER0/7/19) with complementary outputs and dead-time, so the E4 control architecture stands —
only the pin allocation is wrong. HRTIMER faults: FLT0=73(PA12), FLT1=78(PA15), FLT2=47(PB10).

**This blocks fabrication and needs a full re-allocation pass against the datasheet.
Cost impact: none — same part, same package.**

### 3.2 Pins marked no-connect that are functional

| Part | Pin | We had | Datasheet | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NSI6611 ×18 | 12 | NC | **RDY** — active-low open-drain power-good | needs ~5 k pull-up to VCC1; no power-good today |
| NCP1252A | 4 | NC | **RT** — resistor to GND sets fsw (50–500 kHz) | frequency-setting element missing |
| TPS3430 ×2 | 2, 4 | NC | **CWD / CRST** — watchdog timeout & reset delay | watchdog window undefined |
| NSI1042 | 7 | tied to GND1 | **NC** on every variant | **fixed** — now no-connect |

The first three need components added in `cells.tsx` (three resistors, two capacitors) and a
rebuild. Marked in the remap so the next netlist pass cannot lose them. **Cost: < ₹10/module.**

### 3.3 AMC1311 is a single-ended part, drawn as differential

Pin 3 is **SHTDN** (active-high, internal pull-up), not `VINN`; the input is pin 2 only,
referenced to GND1. Because our `VINN` and `GND1` both land on the same node, the circuit is
electrically correct and SHTDN sits low (enabled) — so this does **not** break the measurement.
But pin 6 (**OUTN**) is unused, so the differential output is read single-ended: the
1.39–1.49 V output common-mode then appears as an input-referred error of **≈ ±56 V** on the
3.8 MΩ/6.8 kΩ divider. Fix in firmware calibration or read differentially. **Real, moderate.**

### 3.4 Ratings

| Item | Finding | Fix | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Line CT | 60 A nameplate vs **63.95 A rms/cell at 285 V low line — continuous** | → **100 A** class, burden 33→27 Ω | ~₹0 (same class) — but a 100 A window CT has a different pin pitch, so **catch before layout** |
| DC-link balancers | 1.04 W worst case; a 1 W 2512's √(P·R)=217 V < 220 V at +10 % bus | 2512 **2 W, Umax ≥ 250 V** | ~₹2/module |
| 24 V rail monitor | ADC saturates at **25.74 V** (+7.3 % headroom; +3.3 % worst case) | 68 k → **100 k** | ₹0 |
| HF167F at 60/120 kW | datasheet ceiling is 90 A switching / 100 A carrying; 60 kW needs 101–128 A, 120 kW 182–256 A | price/class already scaled; **part family cannot reach it** — use N×30 kW relay groups or a contactor | already in BOM (₹340/₹520) |
| QA01C-15S18 | **part number does not exist.** Real: QA01C = +20/−4 V, QA01C-18 = +18/−3 V | renamed to `QA01C`; pin map was already right | ₹0 |
| SG2M023120LJ row | LCSC C5713523 is **Wolfspeed C3M0016120K (16 mΩ)**, not the SiChain part | noted as the second source; pinouts identical, no footprint risk | — |

### 3.5 Cooling at 120 kW — the only finding with real money

At 96 % efficiency, 120 kW rejects **5.0 kW**, needing **484 CFM** at a 20 K rise
(3.7 kW/359 CFM at 97 %, 2.4 kW/237 CFM at 98 %). Our fan *class* is already right
(4 × 120×38 PWM, not commodity 120×25) — but the BOM prices them at **₹280 each**, where that
duty (≥200 CFM, ≥300 Pa static, 3 A) is a **₹2,100–3,700** fan.

**Exposure: +₹7,300 to +₹13,700 at 120 kW — 7.7 % to 14.5 % of the ₹94,614 COGS**, against a
red-line already missed by ₹16,614. The header/rail must also carry **3 A per fan, not 1 A**.
This is a price-basis correction, not a design change, and it should go into the next RFQ round.

## 4. Fixed in this pass

- `docs/symbol-pin-map.md` — the verified logical→physical map, published (107 families, 407 pins).
- SKU-honest part numbers: `HF167F-80A/120A/250A-M`, `FUSE-gG-690V-63A/125A/250A`, `CER-50W-AX`
  at 120 kW — the 30 kW label no longer appears on 60/120 kW drawings and BOMs.
- CT 60 A → 100 A class; balance resistors → 2 W/250 V; `QA01C-15S18` → `QA01C`.
- NSI1042 pin 7 no longer tied to GND1.
- The three functional-pin defects recorded in the remap so a rebuild cannot lose them.
- **Costs unchanged**: ₹29,957 / ₹50,780 / ₹94,614 @10k. Every change was a label, a class, or a
  sub-₹10 part — except the fan price risk above, which is quantified, not yet booked.

## 5. Still open

1. **MCU pin re-allocation** (§3.1) — blocks fabrication.
2. Add the RDY pull-up, RT resistor and CWD/CRST timing parts, then rebuild (§3.2).
3. AMC1311 differential read or calibration (§3.3).
4. 60/120 kW input-relay part family (§3.4).
5. Fan price basis and 3 A headers at 120 kW (§3.5).
6. Pre-existing and unchanged: magnetics detail specs, §K datasheet gate, EVT T-01…T-25, PCB layout.

The reviewer's process conclusion stands and is worth keeping: **the next review should be run
against the netlist and this pin map, not the rendered PDF.**
